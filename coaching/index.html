Who is this for?

Coaching built for three types of candidates.

You don't need prior PM or SM experience to start. The coaching is structured around your current situation and target role.

Freshers & Graduates

Starting your career in PM or SM roles

You've completed your degree (engineering, MBA, BCA, or any stream) and want to build a career in project management, Scrum Master, or IT delivery roles — but don't know where to begin.

  • No prior PM experience needed
  • Learn from fundamentals to interview level
  • Understand what the role actually involves day-to-day
  • Build a resume that speaks to PM/SM roles
  • Practice answering "why PM?" and role scenario questions
Career Gap Candidates

Returning after a break — with confidence

You've had a gap of 6 months, 1 year, or longer — due to family, health, relocation, or other personal reasons — and want to re-enter the workforce in a PM or delivery role without the gap holding you back.

  • Refresh and update your project management knowledge
  • Structure your resume to handle the gap professionally
  • Coach you on how to address the gap question in interviews
  • Rebuild confidence to present yourself effectively
  • Prepare for practical scenario questions in the target role
IT Professionals Switching

Moving from Dev / QA / BA to PM or SM

You are currently working as a developer, QA engineer, business analyst, support engineer, or technical lead and want to transition into Scrum Master, Project Manager, Project Coordinator, or Delivery Manager roles.

  • Reframe your existing experience for PM/SM roles
  • Fill gaps in Agile, governance, and delivery knowledge
  • Learn how to position your technical background as a strength
  • Prepare for role-specific scenario and behavioral questions
  • Articulate why you are making the switch — convincingly

Why coaching — not just a YouTube video or Udemy course?

A course teaches. Coaching prepares you.

  • Explains what Scrum or PM is
  • Gives you generic knowledge
  • Cannot hear your answer and give feedback
  • Does not know your background or gap
  • Cannot tell you what is missing in your communication
  • No mock interview or scenario practice
PMAISM Coaching
  • Coaches you on how to answer real interview questions
  • Tailored to your specific role, background, and gap
  • Hears your answers and tells you what is weak or unclear
  • Addresses your career gap or transition story directly
  • Gives specific feedback on communication and content
  • Mock interviews with role-specific scenario questions

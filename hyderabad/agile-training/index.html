Agile Frameworks

Agile Frameworks Covered in Our Hyderabad Training

Scrum

The most widely used Agile framework. PMAISM's Scrum training covers the Scrum Guide, all three roles (Product Owner, Scrum Master, Developer), five events, three artifacts, and real sprint simulation exercises. Prepares candidates for Scrum Master roles and CSM/PSM certification.

Kanban

Kanban is widely used in support, operations, and continuous delivery environments. PMAISM covers Kanban boards, WIP limits, flow metrics, and how Kanban integrates with Scrum in hybrid delivery environments — a frequent interview topic.

Agile Project Management

Blending Agile practices with project governance. Covers hybrid delivery, Agile estimation techniques, release planning, velocity tracking, and how to manage stakeholders in Agile environments — critical for PM and Scrum Master candidates.

SAFe Overview

An introduction to the Scaled Agile Framework for candidates targeting large enterprise environments. Covers Agile Release Trains, PI Planning, and the relationship between team-level Scrum and programme-level delivery in SAFe organisations.

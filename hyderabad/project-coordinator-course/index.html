Hyderabad Job Market

Project Coordinator Opportunities in Hyderabad

Entry-Level PM Roles in Hyderabad

Project Coordinator and Associate PM roles are widely available in Hyderabad's IT corridor. Companies like Infosys, Wipro, HCL, TCS, and hundreds of smaller IT firms regularly hire entry-level PM professionals.

Salary Range for Coordinators in Hyderabad

Project Coordinator salaries in Hyderabad start at ₹4–6 LPA for freshers and grow to ₹8–12 LPA within 2–3 years. Many coordinators move into full PM roles within 18–24 months.

Career Progression

The Project Coordinator role is a well-established stepping stone. Coordinator → Junior PM → Project Manager is the most common progression path in Hyderabad's IT companies. PMAISM coaches you for today's role while keeping the next step in view.

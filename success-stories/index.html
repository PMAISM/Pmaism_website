FAQ

Questions about placements & outcomes.

We provide dedicated placement assistance — resume preparation, LinkedIn optimisation, interview coaching, and referrals. We do not offer a contractual placement guarantee, but our track record of 700+ placed students reflects the depth of support we provide. Most students who complete the full coaching program and follow through on the placement process receive offers within 4–8 weeks.

It varies based on your background, the role you are targeting, and the job market at the time. On average, students with prior IT experience who complete coaching get placed within 4–8 weeks of active interview preparation. Freshers and career gap candidates may take slightly longer — typically 6–12 weeks — depending on how quickly they build confidence and apply consistently. We support you through the entire process, not just the training.

The most common roles our students are placed in include Project Manager, IT Project Manager, Scrum Master, Project Coordinator, IT Project Coordinator, AI Project Manager, Delivery Manager, Program Manager, Release Coordinator, and PMO Analyst. The role depends on your background and the coaching program you complete. Freshers typically start as Project Coordinators, while experienced IT professionals often transition directly into Scrum Master or Project Manager roles.

Students who transition from one IT role to a PM or Scrum Master role typically see salary hikes of 60%–170% on average. The highest individual hike we have recorded is 312%. The average salary range for placed students is ₹12–20 LPA, with the highest package reaching ₹58 LPA. Freshers entering the workforce for the first time or career gap candidates returning after a break typically see the most significant jumps in earnings relative to their previous income or zero income.

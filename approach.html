Approach

A calm, structured way to improve delivery capability.

Every engagement is designed around the client's current maturity, leadership needs, and delivery goals. The emphasis is always practical adoption — not recommendations that stay on paper.

01

Discover

Understand the organization, delivery model, current pain points, stakeholders, team maturity, and the goals driving the engagement. Every effective coaching or consulting engagement starts with listening before advising.

02

Assess

Review Agile practices, PMO maturity, reporting quality, governance discipline, delivery risks, team behaviors, and leadership capability. Identify where the gaps are and what the highest-leverage improvements would be.

03

Design

Create a coaching plan, consulting roadmap, operating cadence, and measurable improvement goals specific to the client's context — not a generic playbook applied without judgment.

04

Coach

Work alongside teams and leaders as they adopt better routines, conversations, and delivery habits. This is where change actually happens — in real work, not in a training room alone.

05

Sustain

Embed practices, transfer capability to internal teams and leaders, and leave the client with a delivery system they can maintain and improve independently after the engagement ends.

Engagement styles

Flexible support for different stages of maturity.

PMAISM adapts the engagement model to what the client actually needs — from focused advisory sessions to sustained coaching relationships.

Advisory

Focused expert guidance for leaders and PMOs making delivery decisions or planning transformation.

Coaching

Hands-on support for teams, Scrum Masters, Product Owners, and managers as they change behaviors.

Workshops

Structured sessions for Agile, PMO, governance, planning, and leadership topics — tailored to the audience.

Assessment

A current-state review with practical recommendations and a clear roadmap for where to improve next.

What You'll Learn

Skills You'll Take Into Your First Scrum Master Role

Every module is mapped to real Scrum Master interview questions and the day-to-day responsibilities hiring managers test for. You won't just know Scrum — you'll be able to demonstrate it.

  • Agile principles and the Scrum Guide — what interviewers actually ask
  • Scrum roles: Product Owner, Scrum Master and Development Team responsibilities
  • Running Sprint Planning, Daily Stand-ups, Reviews and Retrospectives
  • Backlog refinement, story points and velocity-based estimation
  • Impediment removal and servant leadership in practice
  • Stakeholder communication and managing distributed teams

Course Curriculum

6 Modules, Structured for Interview Success

The curriculum is sequenced so each week builds on the last — from foundational Agile knowledge to live mock interview practice. Every module includes exercises, templates and real-world case studies.

01

Agile Foundations

  • History and principles of the Agile Manifesto
  • Waterfall vs Agile vs Hybrid delivery
  • Agile frameworks overview: Scrum, Kanban, SAFe
  • Why organisations adopt Agile and what that means for a Scrum Master
  • Common Agile interview questions and model answers
02

Scrum Framework & Roles

  • The Scrum Guide: pillars, values and accountabilities
  • Product Owner vs Scrum Master vs Developer — clear distinctions
  • Scrum artifacts: Product Backlog, Sprint Backlog, Increment
  • Definition of Done and Definition of Ready
  • Scenarios: what does a Scrum Master do when the team misses a sprint goal?
03

Sprint Ceremonies & Facilitation

  • Sprint Planning: inputs, outputs and facilitation techniques
  • Daily Scrum: anti-patterns and how to run effective stand-ups
  • Sprint Review: demoing work and managing stakeholder expectations
  • Retrospectives: techniques — Start/Stop/Continue, 4Ls, Sailboat
  • Live ceremony role-play with feedback
04

Backlog Management & Estimation

  • Writing and refining user stories with acceptance criteria
  • Backlog grooming and prioritisation frameworks (MoSCoW, WSJF)
  • Story point estimation: Planning Poker, T-shirt sizing
  • Velocity tracking and sprint capacity planning
  • Jira practical: creating epics, stories, tasks and sprint boards
05

Stakeholder & Team Facilitation

  • Servant leadership and coaching mindset
  • Identifying and removing impediments
  • Managing conflict within Scrum teams
  • Communicating sprint progress to senior stakeholders
  • Working with distributed and cross-functional teams
06

Interview Preparation & Mock Sessions

  • Scrum Master resume: structure, keywords, zero-experience framing
  • LinkedIn profile optimisation for Scrum Master visibility
  • Top 50 Scrum Master interview questions with model answers
  • Behavioural questions: STAR method applied to Scrum scenarios
  • Full mock interview with recorded feedback from your coach

Frequently Asked Questions

Common Questions About the Scrum Master Course

Still have questions? Message us on WhatsApp and we'll answer within a few hours.

  • No prior IT experience is required. Our Scrum Master course is designed for freshers, graduates, career-gap candidates and non-IT professionals. We start from Agile fundamentals and build methodically to interview-ready practical skills. If you have never heard of Scrum before, that is a perfectly fine starting point. We have placed students from teaching, banking, retail and hospitality backgrounds into Scrum Master roles after this program.

  • PMAISM offers both online live instructor-led sessions and classroom training in Hyderabad. Online students join live sessions via video conferencing with the same interactive exercises, role-plays and feedback as classroom students. You can choose the format that suits your schedule. Many students in Hyderabad attend classroom for the first few modules and switch to online for the later ones — we accommodate that flexibility.

  • Salary after a Scrum Master course depends on your prior experience and the company you join. Freshers typically start at ₹6–9 LPA. Professionals transitioning from IT roles with 2–5 years of experience often land at ₹12–18 LPA. Our placed students have seen salary hikes ranging from 60% to 170%. The highest package recorded by an PMAISM graduate in a Scrum Master role is ₹58 LPA. We provide salary benchmarking during the interview prep module so you know exactly what to negotiate.

  • Yes. Placement assistance is included for every enrolled student at no extra charge. This includes a professionally rewritten resume tailored to Scrum Master job descriptions, LinkedIn profile optimisation, referrals through our hiring partner network, interview call scheduling support and post-interview debrief sessions. We work with you until you secure a role — our commitment doesn't end on day 42 of the course.

  • The Certified Scrum Master (CSM) certification from Scrum Alliance is a 2-day exam-based credential that tests theoretical knowledge of the Scrum Guide. It is valuable to have on a resume, but it does not prepare you for a Scrum Master job interview or for the realities of the role. PMAISM coaching is interview and role-focused: you practice leading ceremonies, handling difficult team dynamics, managing stakeholders and answering the exact questions interviewers ask. Many of our students pursue CSM after finishing our program and find the exam straightforward because they already have deep practical understanding. If you want a certificate to display, do the CSM. If you want a Scrum Master job, do the PMAISM course first.

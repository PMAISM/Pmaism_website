What You'll Learn

Practical PM Skills Built for Real Interviews and Real Roles

This course goes beyond theory. Every topic is taught through real scenarios and then immediately applied in exercises that mirror what interviewers ask and what hiring managers expect on day one of the job.

  • Project lifecycle — initiation, planning, execution, monitoring and closure
  • Building a project charter, scope document and WBS from scratch
  • Scheduling with Gantt charts, critical path analysis and milestone tracking
  • Risk identification, risk registers and mitigation planning
  • Stakeholder mapping, communication plans and status reporting
  • Agile project delivery: Scrum sprints, Kanban boards, hybrid approaches
  • Budget management and earned value analysis basics
  • PM tools: MS Project, Jira, Asana and practical usage in delivery
  • Managing underperforming teams and difficult stakeholder conversations
  • PM resume, cover letter and full mock interview preparation

Course Curriculum

8 Weeks, 6 Modules — From Fundamentals to Offer Letter

Each module builds on the last. By the end of the course you will have a portfolio of PM artefacts, a polished resume and the confidence to handle any PM interview question thrown at you.

01

Project Management Fundamentals

  • What is a project? Project vs operations vs program
  • PM lifecycle phases: Initiation, Planning, Execution, Monitoring, Closure
  • Project Manager role, responsibilities and day-to-day activities
  • Key PM artefacts: project charter, scope statement, RACI matrix
  • Introduction to PMBOK, PRINCE2 and Agile frameworks — what interviewers ask
02

Planning & Scheduling

  • Work Breakdown Structure (WBS) creation and decomposition
  • Network diagrams, critical path method and float analysis
  • Gantt chart creation and milestone planning in MS Project and Asana
  • Resource allocation, levelling and capacity planning
  • Practical exercise: build a full project plan for a sample IT project
03

Risk & Issue Management

  • Risk identification techniques: brainstorming, SWOT, risk register
  • Probability and impact matrix — qualitative risk assessment
  • Risk response strategies: avoid, mitigate, transfer, accept
  • Issue log management: difference between risks and issues
  • Scenario: how to handle a key vendor going offline mid-project
04

Stakeholder Communication

  • Stakeholder identification and power-interest mapping
  • Communication plan: frequency, channel, format and audience
  • Status reports: executive summary, RAG status, key metrics
  • Managing difficult stakeholders and escalation paths
  • Email and presentation templates for PM communication
05

Agile & Hybrid Delivery

  • When to use Waterfall, Agile or Hybrid — decision framework
  • PM role in Agile environments: working alongside Scrum Masters and POs
  • Kanban for project tracking: board design and WIP limits
  • Hybrid delivery: combining sprint cadence with traditional milestones
  • Reporting to PMO in Agile projects — metrics and governance
06

Interview Preparation

  • PM resume: structure, ATS optimisation and keyword strategy
  • Positioning yourself as a PM with no official PM title
  • Top 60 PM interview questions with model answers
  • Behavioural interview questions using STAR framework
  • Full mock PM interview with recorded feedback and action plan

Who This Course Is For

Freshers & Graduates

No work experience? We teach you how to use your academic projects, internships and extracurricular activities as legitimate PM evidence. Graduates regularly land Project Coordinator and Associate PM roles after this program.

IT Professionals Transitioning to PM

Developers, QAs, system admins and network engineers are ideally positioned to move into PM roles. You already understand delivery, team dynamics and technical constraints. We teach you how to reframe that as PM leadership.

Career Gap Candidates

Whether you took time out for family, health or personal reasons, we coach you on how to address the gap confidently in interviews and use the break as a strength in your narrative. Our career-gap alumni have landed PM roles across India and internationally.

BA / QA Moving Into Delivery

Business analysts and QA engineers frequently transition into PM roles — and PMAISM has helped hundreds do exactly that. We help you build on your existing delivery knowledge and fill the planning, risk and stakeholder gaps that PM interviews probe.

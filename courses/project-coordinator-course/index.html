Project Coordinator Course — Your First Step into Project Management

The PMAISM Project Coordinator course is designed from the ground up for freshers, graduates and career changers with zero PM experience. What You'll Learn

Practical Coordinator Skills That Get You Hired

This course focuses on the exact skills that Project Coordinator job descriptions ask for — not abstract theory. By the end you will have hands-on experience with the tools, documents and communication approaches that real coordinators use every day.

  • What a Project Coordinator does day-to-day and how to explain it in interviews
  • Project lifecycle overview: from kick-off to closure
  • Jira: creating issues, boards, sprints and project tracking dashboards
  • Asana: task management, timelines and project templates
  • MS Project: basic Gantt chart creation and milestone tracking
  • Project documentation: meeting minutes, status reports, action logs
  • RAID log management: Risks, Assumptions, Issues and Dependencies
  • Coordinating between teams: developers, designers, clients and vendors
  • Professional communication: emails, stakeholder updates and meeting facilitation
  • Interview preparation: resume, LinkedIn and mock interview for coordinator roles

Course Curriculum

5 Modules Built for Absolute Beginners

Every module is designed with zero assumptions about prior knowledge. We explain everything from first principles and immediately apply it with practical exercises, real templates and tool walkthroughs.

01

What Does a Project Coordinator Do?

  • Project vs operations — what makes something a project
  • Project Coordinator role: key responsibilities and typical day
  • How a coordinator supports the Project Manager
  • Career path: Coordinator to PM — what the journey looks like
  • Industries that hire coordinators: IT, construction, healthcare, BFSI, consulting
02

PM Tools — Jira, Asana & MS Project

  • Jira fundamentals: projects, boards, issues, epics and sprints
  • Creating and managing a Scrum board in Jira from scratch
  • Asana: workspaces, tasks, timelines, portfolios and reporting
  • MS Project: creating a simple project plan with Gantt and milestones
  • Practical lab: set up and track a sample project in Jira and Asana
03

Documentation & Reporting

  • Meeting minutes: structure, best practices and common mistakes
  • Action log management: tracking decisions and follow-ups
  • Status report writing: RAG status, progress summary, next steps
  • RAID log: maintaining risks, assumptions, issues and dependencies
  • Templates: downloadable coordinator documentation pack
04

Team Coordination & Communication

  • Coordinating between multiple teams without authority
  • Scheduling and facilitating effective project meetings
  • Writing professional emails for project updates and escalations
  • Following up on action items without being seen as pushy
  • Handling conflicting priorities across teams and stakeholders
05

Interview Preparation

  • Project Coordinator resume: structure, keywords and ATS optimisation
  • How to present zero experience convincingly — proven framing techniques
  • Top 40 Project Coordinator interview questions with model answers
  • Behavioural questions: STAR method applied to coordinator scenarios
  • Full mock interview with recorded feedback and personalised action plan

Who This Course Is For

Designed for People Starting From Zero

This is PMAISM's most beginner-friendly program. If you have never worked in a PM role before, this is where you start.

Freshers & Recent Graduates

Just finished your degree and unsure where to start? The Project Coordinator role is one of the best entry points into a professional career in IT and business. We help you land your first role even with no work experience by teaching you how to present your education, academic projects and soft skills as coordinator-relevant evidence.

Non-IT Graduates

Studied commerce, arts, science or any non-CS engineering stream? Project coordination is a role that values communication, organisation and attention to detail far more than technical skills. Many of our most successful placement stories come from non-IT graduates who are now thriving as coordinators at IT companies.

Career Changers

Moving from a completely different field — teaching, retail, hospitality or banking? Your existing skills in communication, scheduling, customer management and team coordination are directly transferable. We help you connect the dots and build the PM-specific knowledge that bridges the gap.

Those With 0 PM Experience

If this is your first exposure to project management, this course is built exactly for you. There are no prerequisites, no assumed knowledge and no prior tools experience required. We teach you everything from first principles and have you confident enough to pass interviews in 6 weeks.

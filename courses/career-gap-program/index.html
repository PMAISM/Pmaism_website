Program Structure

4 Modules — From Assessment to Job-Ready

The program is structured to meet you exactly where you are. We start by understanding your specific situation and end with you walking into interviews fully prepared, with a polished resume and a clear job search plan.

01

Profile Assessment & Gap Analysis

  • One-on-one session to understand your background and the nature of your gap
  • Identifying transferable skills from before and during the gap
  • PM and Scrum knowledge audit — what needs refreshing, what is still strong
  • Defining your re-entry target role and realistic timeline
  • Setting the coaching plan specific to your situation
02

Resume & LinkedIn Rebuilding

  • Resume structure that handles a gap honestly and strategically
  • Rewriting your experience section with achievement-first language
  • ATS keyword optimisation for PM, SM and Coordinator job descriptions
  • Framing the gap period — what to include, what to omit, how to frame it
  • LinkedIn profile update: headline, about section, skills and visibility settings
03

Confidence & Communication Coaching

  • Rebuilding interview confidence after time away from professional settings
  • Managing anxiety and self-doubt that often accompanies a gap
  • Communication clarity: speaking about your experience with conviction
  • Body language, tone and energy in video and in-person interviews
  • Practical exercises to build fluency and confidence before the real interviews
04

Interview Storytelling & Mock Interviews

  • Crafting your gap narrative: a 90-second story that works every time
  • Answering "Why did you take a break?" and "What were you doing during the gap?"
  • STAR method applied to PM and SM scenarios from your past experience
  • Full mock interview: gap questions, behavioural questions, role-specific questions
  • Detailed written feedback and action plan for your next real interview

Who This Program Is For

For Everyone Who Stepped Away and Is Ready to Return

There is no single profile for a career gap candidate. This program has helped people from every background and every reason for their break. What they all have in common is that they are ready to return — and we help them do it right.

Professionals with 1+ Year Career Gap

Whether your gap is one year or five, the challenges of re-entering the job market after an extended break are real. We work with gap candidates of all durations and have placed people back into PM and SM roles regardless of how long they were away.

Mothers Returning to Work

Maternity and child-rearing gaps are among the most common — and most mishandled — gaps in interviews. We specialise in helping mothers returning to work frame their time away with confidence, positioning parenting as evidence of project management, stakeholder management and multitasking capability.

Those Who Took a Break for Health or Family

Health challenges, caregiving for parents or family circumstances — these are valid, human reasons for a gap. You should not have to apologise for them. We coach you on how to acknowledge the gap with dignity and redirect the conversation quickly to your capabilities and readiness.

Professionals Who Left and Want to Re-enter PM

Some people left the workforce to explore other paths, relocate or simply step back. Whatever brought you to a gap, if project management is where you want to return, this program gives you a structured re-entry path with real placement support at the end.

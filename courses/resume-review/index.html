What We Review

Every Element That Determines Whether Your Resume Gets a Call

Most resume reviews are superficial. PMAISM's review goes deep across eight dimensions — the same dimensions that determine whether your resume passes the recruiter screen and the hiring manager read.

  • PM or Scrum Master role alignment — does your resume speak directly to the role?
  • Achievement vs task language — are you showing impact or just listing duties?
  • Keyword optimisation for ATS — are you passing the automated screen?
  • Gap handling and framing — is the career break addressed correctly?
  • Project descriptions and metrics — are your projects described with numbers and outcomes?
  • Action verb strength — are your bullet points starting with strong, varied verbs?
  • Overall format and visual clarity — is the layout clean, scannable and recruiter-friendly?
  • LinkedIn consistency — does your resume align with what recruiters will see on your profile?

Who This Service Is For

Built for Every Stage of the PM Job Search

Whether you are applying for your first PM role or returning after a career break, expert resume feedback at the right moment can be the difference between a call and silence. This service is for anyone who wants to be certain their resume is working as hard as it should.

Candidates Applying for PM/SM/Coordinator Roles

If you are actively applying for project management roles and not getting the call-back rate you expect, your resume is likely the bottleneck. Expert feedback at this stage can significantly increase your screening pass rate and the quality of the roles you are getting called for.

Those with a Career Gap on Their Resume

A gap on a resume requires careful handling — not hiding, but strategic framing. Our experts know exactly how to advise gap candidates on resume structure so the gap is addressed appropriately without becoming the defining feature of the document.

IT Professionals Repositioning Their Resume

Transitioning from developer, QA or BA to PM or Scrum Master? Your resume needs to be reframed — not just reformatted. We identify exactly which of your current experiences can be repositioned as PM-relevant and give you specific language to do that credibly.

Freshers Building Their First PM Resume

No PM experience yet? Your resume still needs to be strong. We help freshers structure a resume that highlights academic projects, internships, certifications and transferable skills in a way that passes the fresher screen for PM and Coordinator entry-level roles.

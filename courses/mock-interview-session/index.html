Mock Interview Session — Real Questions. Real Feedback. Real Confidence.

Most candidates who fail PM and Scrum Master interviews do not fail because of knowledge gaps — they fail because they have never practised answering real interview questions under real interview conditions with someone who can tell them honestly what went wrong. A PMAISM mock interview session changes that. Sixty minutes. Real questions. Detailed written feedback. An action plan you can use before your next real interview.

Session Format

60 Minutes Structured for Maximum Impact

Every minute of the session is purposeful. We do not waste time on pleasantries or generic advice — we get straight into the kind of interview practice that produces measurable improvement.

Part 1

Role-Specific Interview Questions (20 min)

We open with the role-specific technical and knowledge questions that interviewers use to assess whether you actually understand the role. For Scrum Masters: Scrum framework, ceremonies, facilitation and Agile principles. For PMs: planning, risk, stakeholder management, delivery methodology. All delivered in real interview style — no coaching, no hints, just the question and your answer.

Part 2

Behavioural & Situational Questions (20 min)

The behavioural and situational questions that most candidates struggle with — "Tell me about a time when a project went off track," "How do you handle a difficult stakeholder," "What would you do if the team missed the sprint goal?" We probe for STAR-structured answers and test how well you handle pressure, ambiguity and pushback from an interviewer.

Part 3

Feedback, Gaps Identified & Strategy (20 min)

The most valuable part of the session. Your coach gives you honest, specific, written feedback on every answer — what worked, what did not, and exactly how to improve. We identify your top three gaps and build a concrete strategy for your next real interview. You leave with a written action plan, not just encouragement.

What's Covered

Every Dimension of a Real PM or SM Interview

The session covers all the elements that real interviewers assess — not just what you know, but how you communicate it, how you handle pressure and whether you come across as someone a hiring manager would trust with their projects.

  • Scrum Master-specific questions: framework, ceremonies, impediments, team dynamics
  • PM behavioural questions: planning, risk, stakeholder management, delivery pressure
  • Coordinator scenario questions: task coordination, communication, priority management
  • Agile knowledge gaps — what you do not know but need to know for the interview
  • Communication and clarity coaching — how you say things, not just what you say
  • How to structure answers using the STAR method for PM and SM scenarios
  • What interviewers actually look for — and what causes them to reject candidates
  • A personalised action plan for your next real interview

Who This Session Is For

For Candidates Who Are Close but Have Not Crossed the Line Yet

A mock interview session is most powerful when you are actively in the job search process and need to convert your interview calls into offers. These are the candidates who benefit most.

Candidates with 1–3 Active Interview Processes

If you have interview calls coming up and want to walk in as prepared as possible, a PMAISM mock session is the best preparation you can do. The feedback you get will be directly applicable to your real interviews — sometimes within days of the mock session itself.

Those Who Have Failed PM or SM Interviews Before

Failed an interview and don't know exactly why? That is one of the most common and frustrating experiences in the PM job search. A mock session gives you the honest, detailed feedback that real interviewers never give you — so you know precisely what went wrong and what to do differently next time.

Candidates About to Start Their Job Search

Not yet in interviews but about to start applying? A mock session at this stage shows you which gaps to close before the real calls start coming. It is far better to discover your weaknesses in a practice session than in front of a panel at a company you really want to join.

Anyone Who Wants Expert Interview Feedback

You can be at any stage of your career — fresher, experienced PM, career gap candidate or someone returning from a break. If you want the honest expert perspective on how your interview performance comes across, this session delivers exactly that.

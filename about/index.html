About

Built for organizations and professionals that want delivery improvement to last.

PMAISM is a coaching and consulting firm focused on helping teams and professionals improve project delivery capability — across Agile, PMO, Scrum, delivery leadership, and role-based interview preparation.

Who we are

"A partner for teams and leaders doing complex delivery work."

PMAISM supports organizations that want to improve how work is planned, governed, executed, and learned from. Our work is grounded in real delivery realities: shifting priorities, cross-functional dependencies, stakeholder pressure, and teams that need practical guidance.

The firm also works directly with students and professionals who are building careers in project management, Agile, and delivery — coaching them through the practical knowledge and interview skills needed to succeed in competitive hiring processes.

PMAISM focuses on building capability, not dependency. A consulting engagement should leave the organization with stronger routines, clearer governance, and more confident leaders. A coaching program should leave the individual ready to perform in the role — not just pass an interview.

Best fit for
  • Organizations starting or improving Agile adoption
  • PMOs that need better governance and executive reporting
  • Delivery teams facing delays, unclear ownership, or weak planning
  • Leaders who want practical coaching for project execution
  • Students and professionals preparing for PM, SM, or Coordinator roles
  • Candidates targeting Delivery Manager and Program Manager interviews

Founder

Bhushan Jha

Founder & Head Coach — PMAISM, Hyderabad

In Agile delivery, Scrum coaching, and project management, Bhushan founded PMAISM to bring the kind of practical, hands-on coaching that corporate teams take for granted — to freshers, career gap candidates, and IT professionals who want to move into delivery roles.

His coaching is grounded in how real delivery environments work, not in textbook theory. Over 700 professionals have gone through his programs — across Scrum Master, Project Manager, Project Coordinator, and Delivery Manager roles — with students achieving packages up to ₹58 LPA and salary hikes of 60–170%.

His approach is direct: understand where you are right now, close the gaps that actually matter for your target role, and prepare you for how interviews run in practice — including how to handle career gaps, lateral moves, and the questions most candidates stumble on.
